One thing worth knowing. No sound can play before a guest touches the screen — every browser blocks it, and the opening screen is before that first touch. So music on the opening screen would stay silent for most people. That is why Option 1 is a flourish at the moment the doors part, rather than a song before it: the tap is what unlocks the audio.
